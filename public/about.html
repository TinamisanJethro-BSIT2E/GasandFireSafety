About the system

Department of Hospitality Management · Cavite State University – Imus Campus

IoT-Based Gas Leakage and Fire Safety System with Automatic Control

This prototype monitors LPG using an MQ-6 sensor and detects flame using a flame sensor. The ESP32 evaluates sensor data locally: gas leaks trigger regulator shut-off, exhaust ventilation, and an audible alarm; fire detection prioritizes the fire status and activates the sprinkler pump while shutting off gas. The 16×2 I²C LCD and GSM modem provide local status and SMS alerts.

Safety design: monitoring and web connectivity are supplementary. The ESP32 runs detection logic independently using firmware-configured thresholds and delay. Validate every sensor, relay, valve, and power design under supervised conditions before any real-world installation.

System block diagram

A regulated supply powers the ESP32, sensors, display, modem, and actuator power stages. Size supply and relay drivers for the actual loads.

Wiring diagram (functional)

Relay modules must be suitable for the load and isolated as appropriate. Do not power pumps, fans, or SIM800L directly from ESP32 pins. Verify module voltage levels and common-ground requirements.
